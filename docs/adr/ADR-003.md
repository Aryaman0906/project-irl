# ADR-003: Evidence confidence rather than lie detection

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-30

## Context
Project IRL serves young users and must remain safe, understandable, evolvable, and honest about what technology can observe.

## Decision
Classify legitimately observable evidence; never claim truth, honesty, or morality.

## Alternatives Considered
Truth scores; intrusive surveillance.

## Consequences
The boundary is explicit and testable, at the cost of accepting honest capability limits and some adapter/policy work.

## Privacy/Safety Impact
Reduces surveillance, overclaiming, coercion, and inappropriate automated authority over young users.

## Migration Impact
Future implementations must preserve this boundary; changes require a superseding ADR and migration plan.

## Related SRS Requirements
FR-036–FR-042; NFR-012

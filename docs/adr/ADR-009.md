# ADR-009: Separate prototype XP, optional services, and valuable rewards

- **Status:** Accepted for Phase 0.1 research only
- **Date:** 2026-10-03

## Context
Phase 0.1 tests motivation and commercial comprehension without creating a financial incentive to misreport offline behaviour. The SRS requires non-transferable, non-cash baseline points (FR-042–FR-044) and ADR-004 bars valuable rewards for self-reported quests.

## Decision
Maintain three separate concepts:

1. **XP** is locally stored, non-cash participation progress. It is the only thing earned in this prototype and cannot be transferred, spent, redeemed, or promised future conversion.
2. A **possible future subscription** would provide optional services or content. Core participation, history, privacy controls, and accumulated XP cannot be paywalled. It cannot increase XP rates, caps, or storage capacity.
3. A **possible future valuable reward entitlement** would be a separately funded and governed ledger with approved evidence, requirements, legal review, backend integrity, supplier obligations, and an ADR. It does not exist here. Self-report cannot create it.

Research selections express local preferences only and activate no entitlement. Deterministic `ProgressService` owns attempts and XP; UI widgets cannot award either.

## Alternatives Considered
A spendable coin; paid XP multipliers; converting XP later; valuable rewards for self-report. All were rejected because they blur claims, create gaming incentives, or introduce unapproved financial obligations.

## Consequences
Concept previews cannot test checkout or willingness to pay. They can test comprehension and category interest. XP remains motivational rather than financial.

## Privacy/Safety Impact
No payment, address, identity, merchant, or delivery data is collected. Local preferences are deleted by the same reset as attempts and reflections.

## Migration Impact
Legacy completed quest IDs become completed attempts with stable `legacy-<quest-id>` IDs and no retroactive XP. Existing valid v2 awards remain authoritative and idempotent. Commercial work requires a new production design; prototype balances must not migrate to valuable entitlements.

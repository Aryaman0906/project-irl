# Phase-0 Hypotheses

These are unvalidated research hypotheses, not product claims.

- **H1:** Users understand a “quest” as a short real-world activity.
- **H2:** Users can move from app to offline task without continuous interaction.
- **H3:** Users distinguish observed/device evidence from self-report.
- **H4:** Structured reflection is low-friction after offline activity.
- **H5:** Users prefer choosing among a small number of quests over one forced assignment.
- **H6:** Personal progress can motivate without becoming a morality or competition score.

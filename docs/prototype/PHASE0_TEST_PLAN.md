# Phase-0 Research Test Plan

Use consented, facilitated sessions with synthetic profiles. Do not record audio/video or collect identifiable telemetry by default.

| Scenario | Target observation | Success evidence | Failure evidence | Feedback questions | Minimal metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explain “quest” then choose a goal | Meaning and navigation are clear | Participant paraphrases and selects unaided | Confuses quest with game/score or stalls | “What do you expect next?” | Help requests; selection time |
| Compare three quest options | Choice feels useful, not forced | Gives a reason and can skip/back | Feels judged or options seem unsafe | “Why this one? What would you change?” | Options viewed; selection/skip |
| Read details and start | Duration and offline expectation are clear | Can explain timer's limited meaning | Believes timer proves task completion | “What will the app know?” | Comprehension response |
| Leave app, then complete/cancel | Loop supports real-world departure | Returns and deliberately completes/cancels | Watches timer or fears cancellation | “Did the app pull you back?” | Session outcome; elapsed range |
| Structured reflection | Low-friction learning capture | Completes optional choices comfortably | Wants to avoid or feels graded | “Did any answer feel judgmental?” | Completion/abandonment; choices |
| Evidence explanation | Labels are honest and understood | Distinguishes self-report from observation | Interprets label as honesty proof | “What exactly was verified?” | Correct explanation count |
| Progress screen | Progress is calm and secondary | Understands count without pressure | Treats count as worth/rank/streak debt | “How would missing a day feel?” | Screen dwell; pressure rating |

No invasive analytics are required. Facilitators may record anonymous task completion, elapsed bands, observed confusion, voluntary ratings, and de-identified notes. Do not infer honesty or psychological traits.

Phase 0.1 reliability and physical-device procedures are maintained in `PHASE01_PILOT_PROTOCOL.md`. Core checks must pass before concept previews are shown.

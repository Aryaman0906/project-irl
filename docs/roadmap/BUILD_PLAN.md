# Project IRL Build Plan

The SRS is authoritative. Delivery advances only after review of the preceding stage. Production uses complete vertical slices, beginning with Focus goal → curated quest → offline attempt → deterministic evidence classification → append-only ledger → structured reflection → progress. Building disconnected UI/backend/AI layers is not the default.

## Phase-0 validation questions

Do users understand quests and goal selection? Are lengths appropriate? Why do they skip? Is reflection low-friction? Are evidence labels understood? Does the app encourage leaving the screen? Does progress remain secondary rather than becoming a pressure score?

## Spike backlog

- **SPIKE-ANDROID-USAGE:** Flutter/Kotlin and UsageStats feasibility.
- **SPIKE-IOS-DEVICE-ACTIVITY:** entitlement and practical Family Controls/Device Activity limits.
- **SPIKE-PERMISSION-LOSS:** permission removal during a session.
- **SPIKE-OFFLINE-RESUME:** robust restart/offline recovery.
- **SPIKE-SYNC-IDEMPOTENCY:** duplicate-safe completion synchronization.
